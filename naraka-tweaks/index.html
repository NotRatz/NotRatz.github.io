Knowledge base

Need extra context? Expand FAQs sourced from Team Strive analysts. Motion is minimal to keep reading comfortable.

How do presets sync across devices?

Presets are stored locally first. When you authenticate with Discord in serverless mode, your namespace syncs to Workers KV and merges automatically. Conflicts prompt you inside the preset manager.

Can I revert everything quickly?

Each tweak has a revert script. Use the multi-select to create a "Restore" preset before a LAN so you can roll back in one click.

Is there telemetry?

No tracking scripts, no analytics. Metrics live client-side only. Opt into anonymous export if you want to share aggregated settings with staff.
