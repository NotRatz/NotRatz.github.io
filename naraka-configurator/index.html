Setting guides

            
Quick refreshers on what matters for performance, clarity, and responsiveness.

          

        

        

          

            
Graphic Quality Level

            
Controls core visuals: geometry, textures, shadows, volumetrics, and post-processing. Higher values look richer but cost GPU time.

          

          

            
System Quality Setting

            
Resolution scaling, upscalers, framerate caps, V-Sync, HDR, and competitive toggles like NVIDIA Reflex or AMD Anti-Lag.

          

          

            
Upscaler Modes

            
DLSS, XeSS, and FSR modes balance sharpness against performance. Choose Automatic for sensible defaults or Quality for LAN play.

          

          

            
Accessibility

            
Color blindness profiles and strength controls improve readability for different vision needs without affecting FPS.

          

          

            
Ray Tracing & Style

            
Luxury options for showmatches. Disable for scrims to keep frametimes stable unless you showcase hardware.

          

          

            
Presets & Sharing

            
Use the preset chips for instant tuning, then copy the JSON to sync teammates. Local storage remembers your last edits.
